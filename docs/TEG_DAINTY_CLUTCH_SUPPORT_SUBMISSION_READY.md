## Short bio / pitch
KAM DRIDI is an independent Canadian hard rock / cinematic rock artist from Montreal, currently developing an Australian live and release campaign for early 2027. The live show is a deliberately compact solo production: lead vocal performed live over professionally prepared stereo backing tracks, with no touring band or backline required. A 20–35 minute original set can be changed over quickly and expanded with synchronized visuals/lighting where venue production allows. The current featured track, “War Machines”, combines hard-rock weight with cinematic and industrial textures and is a strong audience fit for the Clutch / Corrosion Of Conformity tour.

## Why this tour
KAM DRIDI would like to be considered for any suitable opening/support opportunity on the **Clutch Australia 2027** headline run. The project can travel with an extremely small footprint and is available for one suitable date or multiple dates depending on terms and routing.
