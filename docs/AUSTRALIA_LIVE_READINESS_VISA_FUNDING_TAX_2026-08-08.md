## 2. Australian performance visa — working assumption to verify after a real offer
Australian Department of Home Affairs guidance says performers/entertainers intending to work in the entertainment industry in Australia may be eligible for the Temporary Activity visa (subclass 408) in an entertainment activity.

The subclass 400 Short Stay Specialist visa explicitly does **not** allow a person to perform as an entertainer in Australia; Home Affairs directs entertainers who will perform toward subclass 408 Entertainment instead.

Operational rule:
- Do not enter Australia intending to perform on a visitor/promotional-only basis without verifying the correct work authorization.
- Once a real show/contract/invitation exists, obtain the promoter/venue support documents and verify the exact subclass 408 entertainment requirements current at that date.

## 3. Funding — CALQ Circulation d’œuvres hors Québec
Current CALQ program:
- supports music and other artistic disciplines for circulation of performances outside Quebec/internationally;
- applications accepted at any time;
- must be filed at least four weeks before the project;
- requires written confirmation from at least one recognized presenting venue/organization specifying date and hosting conditions;
- maximum support: up to 75% of total project cost;
- listed eligible expenses include travel and visa-related costs, accommodation/per diem (subject to program maximums), equipment transport, equipment rental, promotion/publicity not covered by host, agency fees and limited administration costs;
- salaries/cachets and presentation fees are listed as ineligible expenses;
- professional-artist eligibility and other program conditions still need to be satisfied.

## 7. Australian payment / tax withholding
Australian Taxation Office guidance states that payments to foreign residents for entertainment activities, including musical performances and performance fees, can be subject to Australian PAYG foreign-resident withholding.

The ATO also provides a process for foreign resident payees to request a variation/reduction of withholding in qualifying circumstances.

Manager rule for every offer:
1. Ask whether quoted fee is **gross or net** of Australian withholding/taxes.
2. Ask who is responsible for withholding/remittance.
3. Ask what payment summary/receipt will be supplied.
4. Confirm currency (AUD/CAD/etc.), payment method and due date.
5. Do not promise an ABN/TFN or tax status without professional/current verification.
6. If the amount is material, verify Canada–Australia tax treatment with a qualified tax professional before contract acceptance.
