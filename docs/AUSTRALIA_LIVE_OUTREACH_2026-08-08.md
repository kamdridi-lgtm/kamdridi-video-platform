## Commercial rule
Prefer paid shows. Always ask about artist fee, door split, travel support, accommodation, per diem, technical support and payment timing before accepting.

An unpaid show may still be considered when the total cost is low and the strategic value is strong: credible venue/festival, real audience, professional video/photo opportunity, media/network value, or the ability to build several nearby dates around it.

Do not sign contracts, accept exclusivity, commit rights, buy travel, spend money, disclose banking/passport information, or make legal/visa representations without explicit artist approval.

## Manager workflow when a reply arrives
For every real show opportunity:
1. Verify promoter/venue/contact.
2. Confirm date, city, set length, billing position and audience context.
3. Ask what they pay: guaranteed fee, door split, travel, hotel, per diem, local transport.
4. Confirm currency, payment method and payment timing.
5. Confirm PA, microphone, stereo playback input, monitor, soundcheck/changeover.
6. Ask about screen/video and lighting control if useful.
7. Check artist visa/work-permit implications before committing.
8. Review contract, cancellation terms, exclusivity, rights and publicity obligations.
9. Calculate total out-of-pocket cost versus cash + strategic value.
10. Tell Karim exactly what he personally needs to do next.
